AI site chat that hands off to Google Chat

Visitors get instant answers from a text file you control. When the AI can't help, the chat lands in your team's Google Chat space and your replies go straight back to the visitor.

Try the bar at the bottom. Click Point, then click anything on this page and ask about it.

How it works

  1. The AI answers first. Cloudflare Workers AI replies using only what's in knowledge.txt, with Gemini as an optional fallback.
  2. It hands off when it should. Asking for a person, or anything the knowledge doesn't cover, pings your Google Chat space in a thread for that visitor.
  3. Your team replies from Google Chat. @mention the app in the thread and the reply shows up in the visitor's chat within seconds.

Limits worth knowing

  • Team replies must @mention the Chat app, in the visitor's thread. Google Chat only sends @mentioned messages to apps.
  • If your Pages project ships a _worker.js (next-on-pages does), Cloudflare ignores the functions folder.
  • Replies arrive by polling, so expect a few seconds of delay.

Drop it into your site

<script src="/chat-widget.js" defer
  data-name="Acme"
  data-email="hello@acme.com"></script>

Copy functions/ to your Pages project root and chat-widget.js to your static folder. The README walks through the rest in about 10 minutes.
